Namco Classic Collection Vol.1
(Namco, 1995)

Galaga, Xevious and Mappy (Original
and Arrangement) on Namco's ND-1
board, all in gateware:

* 68000 at 12.288 MHz, game logic
* H8/3002 at 16.384 MHz sub CPU,
  inputs and sound driver
* Namco C352 32-voice PCM
* Yamaha YGV608 tilemap/sprite VDP
* 288x224 at 59.92 Hz, rotated

Controls
  D-pad / stick   move
  A               button 1
  B               button 2
  X or Y          button 3
  Select          coin
  Start           start
  (a second pad plays player 2)
Settings and records are kept in the
EEPROM and saved to ncv1.sav.

ROMs are not included. Build the
image from your own MAME ncv1 romset:

  python3 mra_build.py ncv1.mra
      ncv1.zip

and copy ncv1.rom into
Assets/ncv1/common/.
