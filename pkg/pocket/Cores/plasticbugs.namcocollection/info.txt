Namco Classic Collection
Vol.1 (1995) and Vol.2 (1996)

Both collections on Namco's ND-1
board, all in gateware:

  Vol.1  Galaga, Xevious, Mappy
  Vol.2  Pac-Man, Rally-X, Dig Dug

each in Original and Arrangement.

* 68000 at 12.288 MHz, game logic
* H8/3002 at 16.384 MHz sub CPU,
  inputs and sound driver
* Namco C352 32-voice PCM
* Yamaha YGV608 tilemap/sprite VDP
* 288x224 at 59.92 Hz, rotated

Controls
  D-pad / stick   move
  A               button 1
  B               button 2
  X               button 3
  Select          coin
  Start           1 player start
  R               2 player start
  A second pad plays player 2, or
  turn on Pass-and-Play to play
  both players on this one.

Settings and records are kept in the
EEPROM, saved per collection to
ncv1.sav / ncv2.sav.

ROMs are not included. Build each
image from your own MAME romset:

  python3 mra_build.py ncv1.mra
      ncv1.zip
  python3 mra_build.py ncv2.mra
      ncv2.zip

and copy ncv1.rom / ncv2.rom into
Assets/namcocollection/common/.
